Look at any massage listing in London and you will see two words again and again: **in-call** and **outcall**. They are simple, but they are rarely explained — and they change everything about how a session feels, what it costs, and how private it is.

Here is the difference in plain English, an honest look at the pros and cons of each, and the reasons I decided to work in-call only.

## In-Call vs Outcall: The Simple Definition

**In-call massage** means *you go to the therapist*. You travel to her own studio or treatment room, where everything is already set up.

**Outcall massage** means *the therapist comes to you* — to your home or your hotel room — bringing what she needs with her.

That is the whole distinction. Everything else follows from it.

## The Case for Outcall

To be fair to it: outcall has one real advantage, and it is a good one. **You do not have to go anywhere.** After the massage you are already home, or already in your hotel room, and you can simply stay in the feeling.

For some people — those who cannot travel easily, or who are in London for one night with no time to spare — that convenience is worth paying for.

The trade-offs are that it costs more, the therapist can only bring so much with her, and the quality of the session depends on your space: how warm the room is, how quiet the building is, and whether you can truly relax in a place where your laptop is on the desk and your phone is on the bedside table.

## The Case for In-Call

An in-call studio exists for one purpose. That is its whole advantage.

- **The room is right every time.** The temperature, the lighting, the music, the fresh linen — all of it is prepared before you arrive, and none of it depends on you.
- **Everything is on hand.** Warm oils, a shower before and after, and for a [nuru massage](/services/nuru-massage), the gel, warm water and a room set up for it. Nuru in particular is very difficult to do properly anywhere else.
- **It costs less.** There is no travel time or taxi fare built into the price.
- **You can actually switch off.** There is something about leaving your own space — and your own to-do list — that lets the mind go quiet. Many of my clients say the short journey is part of the ritual.
- **It is more discreet.** Nobody visits your home. No hotel reception sees a visitor. You simply go out for an hour.

## Why I Only Offer In-Call

I am sometimes asked whether I can come to a hotel. The honest answer is no — I do not offer outcall at all — and these are my reasons.

**I want every session to be my best.** In my own studio I control everything that makes a massage good. In someone else's room I control almost nothing. I would rather give one kind of session, well, than two kinds, unevenly.

**The massages I specialise in need the right space.** [Tantric massage](/services/tantric-massage) depends on warmth, quiet and time. [Nuru](/services/nuru-massage) and [body to body massage](/services/body-to-body-massage) need a proper set-up and a shower close by. They are not styles that travel well.

**It keeps things calm and private for both of us.** I work alone, in a place I know. You visit a studio whose address is shared only with people who have booked. That is the most discreet arrangement there is.

**It keeps my prices honest.** No travel supplements, no minimum bookings, no surcharges. My [price list](/pricing) is the same for everyone.

## How to Choose

Choose **outcall** if convenience matters more to you than anything else, and you have a warm, quiet, private space.

Choose **in-call** if you want the best version of the massage itself, a lower price, and complete privacy — and you do not mind a short journey to get it.
