Of all the treatments I offer, [body to body massage](/services/body-to-body-massage) is the one clients find hardest to ask about. The messages usually arrive carefully worded: *what exactly does it involve? Is it like a normal massage? What should I expect?*

Fair questions, all of them. So here is the calm, honest guide I wish everyone could read before their first session — no mystique, no coyness, just what a body to body erotic massage in London actually is.

## What "Body to Body" Actually Means

The name is refreshingly literal. In a body to body — or B2B — massage, the therapist massages you not only with her hands, but with her entire body. After beginning like a classic oil massage, the session gradually deepens: warm oil, skin against skin, long gliding movements performed with the whole body rather than palms and fingers alone.

It is the most intimate form of erotic massage — and, at the same time, one of the most genuinely relaxing things you can experience. Those two facts are not in tension. The continuous, enveloping contact quiets the nervous system in a way that isolated touch simply cannot. Clients often tell me they stopped being able to think about anything at all somewhere in the first twenty minutes. That is the point.

## How a Session Unfolds

Every therapist works differently; here is how it goes in my [West Kensington studio](/massage-west-london).

You arrive to a warm, candlelit room — never rushed, never clinical. A hot shower first, always. Then the massage begins conventionally: warm oil, slow strokes, shoulders and back, your breathing gradually settling.

The transition into body to body is gradual rather than abrupt. The pressure becomes broader, warmer, more enveloping as hands give way to forearms, and forearms to full contact. There is no fixed choreography — I follow your breathing and how your body responds. By the second half of the session, most clients are somewhere between deep relaxation and a kind of pleasant weightlessness.

The session ends the way it began: slowly, with no abrupt stop, and another shower if you wish. You will want a few quiet minutes before rejoining the world. Build them in.

## B2B, Nuru, Sensual — Which One Is for You?

The sensual styles form a family, and it helps to know the differences:

- **[Sensual massage](/services/sensual-massage)** — the foundation. Slow, teasing, hands-led full-body massage with warm oil. The perfect first step if full contact feels like a leap.
- **Body to body** — everything above, performed with the whole body. Maximum contact, maximum immersion.
- **[Nuru massage](/services/nuru-massage)** — the Japanese cousin. A special seaweed gel, extraordinarily slippery, traditionally performed with the whole body on a prepared surface. The most unusual sensation of the three — I have written a [full nuru guide](/blog/nuru-body-to-body-massage-london) if you are curious.
- **[Tantric massage](/services/tantric-massage)** — less about contact style, more about pace and breath. Slow, meditative, ritual-like.

If you are undecided, tell me what draws you when you [message me](/booking) — helping you choose is part of the job, and there is no wrong answer.
