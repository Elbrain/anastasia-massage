Westminster is not a neighbourhood that relaxes easily. Between Parliament, Whitehall, Victoria Street and the offices around St James's Park, SW1 runs on long days, short lunch breaks and a great deal of sitting in meetings.

Which is exactly why so many people who work here go looking for a massage — and why it helps to know what is actually on offer.

## Massage Options in and Around SW1

**Hotel spas.** The grand hotels around Westminster and Victoria have spas. They are polished and convenient, priced accordingly, and usually close by early evening.

**Gyms and clinics.** Several of the area's gyms and health clubs have treatment rooms, and there are physiotherapy and osteopathy clinics around Victoria. These are practical and efficient — good for a specific ache, less so if what you want is to switch off.

**Independent therapists.** There are fewer private studios in SW1 itself than you might expect; rents see to that. Most people who want an unhurried, personal session travel a few stops for it.

## The Westminster Problem: Switching Off

The people I see from this part of London tend to arrive the same way: shoulders up, phone still in hand, mind still in the last meeting. They do not need a complicated treatment. They need an hour in which nobody wants anything from them.

That is harder to find in a spa next door to the office than somewhere a short journey away. A little distance helps. By the time you step off the train, the day has already started to loosen its grip.
