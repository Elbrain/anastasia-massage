Almost everyone is a little nervous before their first sensual massage. That is not a sign you should not go — it is simply what happens when you do something new, private and unhurried in a life that is usually none of those things.

So here is the whole thing, start to finish, with nothing left vague: how to book, how to prepare, what actually happens in the room, and the simple etiquette that makes it easy for both of us. I am Anna, and I give [sensual](/services/sensual-massage), [tantric](/services/tantric-massage), [nuru](/services/nuru-massage) and [body to body massage](/services/body-to-body-massage) from my private studio in West Kensington — so this is exactly what I wish every first-time visitor knew.

## The Short Version

A first sensual massage looks like this:

1. You send a short message to book, and agree the massage, the time and the price.
2. You arrive freshly showered, on time, with the exact amount in cash.
3. We have a relaxed chat, and you can shower at the studio if you like.
4. The massage begins slowly, with warm oil, in a warm, candlelit room — and builds at your pace.
5. It winds down gently, you take your time, and you leave feeling lighter than you arrived.

That really is all. The rest of this guide simply fills in the detail.

## Before You Book: Choosing a Massage

You do not need to understand every style before you get in touch. But it helps to know the basic differences:

- **[Sensual massage](/services/sensual-massage)** — slow, hands-led, warm oil. The gentlest introduction, and what I recommend for most first visits.
- **[Relaxing massage](/services/relaxing-massage)** — softer still; purely about switching off.
- **[Tantric massage](/services/tantric-massage)** — slow, breath-led and meditative. Lovely for a first visit if your mind never stops.
- **[Body to body](/services/body-to-body-massage) and [nuru massage](/services/nuru-massage)** — given with the whole body rather than the hands alone. More immersive; often what people try second.

If you want the full comparison, I have written a guide to [every type of erotic massage in London](/blog/erotic-massage-types-tantric-nuru-naturist-london). And if you are still unsure, just say so when you message — helping people choose is part of my job.

## How to Book (and What to Say)

I take bookings by WhatsApp or phone. A good first message is short and clear. Something like:

*"Hi Anna, I'd like to book a one-hour sensual massage this Thursday around 7pm if you have space. It's my first visit."*

That tells me everything I need: **which massage, how long, and when**. Telling me it is your first time is genuinely useful — it means I will explain a little more and start a little slower.

A few things that make booking smooth:

- **Agree everything up front.** The massage, the length and the price are settled when you book. My [prices](/pricing) are published and they are exactly what you pay.
- **Use plain, polite language.** You are booking a massage from a menu. Messages that are respectful get a warm, quick reply.
- **Book ahead for evenings and weekends.** Same-day appointments are often possible, but those slots go first.

Once we have agreed a time, I send the exact address and simple directions. My studio is in West Kensington (W14), a few minutes from West Kensington and Barons Court stations.

## How to Prepare on the Day

Preparation is simple, and it is mostly about arriving comfortable.

- **Shower before you come.** Fresh, clean skin is the single most important courtesy — and it makes the massage feel better for you, too. You are also welcome to shower at the studio before we start.
- **Arrive at the agreed time, not early.** A private studio has no reception or waiting room. Arriving exactly on time keeps things relaxed and discreet for everyone.
- **Bring the exact amount in cash.** I accept cash only. A plain envelope is a nice touch.
- **Eat lightly and skip the alcohol.** A heavy meal or a few drinks beforehand dulls the experience. Arrive clear-headed and you will enjoy it far more.
- **Leave time afterwards.** Do not book something stressful straight after. Give yourself half an hour to stay in the feeling.

If you are running late, just message me. It happens, and a quick note is always appreciated.

## What Happens When You Arrive

You will be welcomed in, and we will have a short, relaxed chat: how you are feeling, what you would like from the session, anything you would like me to know. This is also when you hand over the envelope — at the start, without being asked, so neither of us has to think about it again.

Then you can shower, and I will show you to the room. It is warm, softly lit, clean and quiet, with fresh linen and towels. You get comfortable, and we begin.

## During the Massage

A first session always starts gently — usually with slow, warm-oil strokes across your back and shoulders — because your body needs a few minutes to trust that it is allowed to relax. From there the massage flows across the whole body and builds gradually.

Three things I want every first-timer to know:

**You are in control.** If you would like a lighter or firmer touch, a slower pace, or a moment to pause, just say so. You will not offend me. I would much rather know.

**You do not have to talk — or stay silent.** Some people chat for the first few minutes and then drift off. Some say nothing at all. Some fall asleep. All of it is fine.

**The session has a clear shape.** What I offer is exactly what is on the [menu](/services). Respecting that is what keeps the experience relaxed and safe for both of us — and it is why you can let go completely, knowing precisely what to expect.

## Etiquette: The Do's and Don'ts

Sensual massage etiquette is really just good manners.

**Do:**

- Arrive clean, on time and sober
- Agree the session and the price when you book
- Pay at the start, in cash
- Say what you like — pace, pressure, anything that would help you relax
- Treat the therapist with the warmth and respect you would like in return

**Don't:**

- Turn up early, late without a message, or unannounced
- Try to renegotiate the price or the session once you arrive
- Ask for anything that is not on the menu
- Take photos, or share the address with anyone

Follow those and you will be welcome back anywhere.

## Afterwards

There is no rush to leave the table. When the massage ends, take a few moments, then shower if you would like to and dress in your own time. Most people leave feeling calm, loose and pleasantly quiet-minded — and sleep unusually well that night.

Drink some water, be gentle with the rest of your day, and notice how you feel. Many first-time visitors tell me afterwards that they wish they had come sooner.
