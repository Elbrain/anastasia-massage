"West London" covers a lot of ground. It runs from the stucco terraces of Kensington and Notting Hill, through the riverside streets of Hammersmith and Fulham, out to the village greens of Chiswick. Each area has its own pace — and its own kind of massage scene.

I work in the middle of it, in West Kensington, so here is a local's guide.

## Inner West London

### Kensington and Chelsea

The most polished end of the market. Hotel spas, day spas and beauty clinics are everywhere, and prices are the highest in West London. Wonderful for an occasion; less personal if you want a regular therapist. See my guides to [massage in Kensington](/blog/massage-kensington) and [massage in Chelsea](/blog/massage-chelsea).

### Notting Hill and Holland Park

A little more relaxed and boutique in feel, with small studios and wellness spaces among the cafés and shops. I have written a separate [guide to massage in Notting Hill](/blog/massage-notting-hill).

### Earl's Court and West Kensington

The practical heart of inner West London: brilliantly connected, full of hotels and mansion flats, and noticeably less expensive than its neighbours. This is where my studio is.

## Further West

### Hammersmith

A major transport hub with a big office population. Massage here leans towards gyms, physiotherapy clinics and after-work appointments. It is one stop from my studio.

### Fulham

Residential, sociable and very active — a lot of runners, cyclists and gym-goers, and plenty of sports-minded clinics to match. North End Road leads straight from Fulham up to West Kensington.

### Chiswick, Shepherd's Bush and beyond

Chiswick has a village feel and a good number of local therapists and clinics along the High Road. Shepherd's Bush is busier and more mixed. Both are within easy reach of West Kensington — Chiswick is a direct District line ride.

## What to Look For, Wherever You Are

West London has every kind of massage at every price. A few things hold true across all of it:

- **Published prices** are a sign of a professional. Vague pricing is a sign of the opposite.
- **Independent therapists** usually offer more time and attention than a busy spa.
- **A short journey is worth it** for the right person. Ten minutes on the Tube is nothing against a session you actually enjoy.
