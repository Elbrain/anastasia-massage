Tantric, nuru, naturist, body to body, sensual — if you have ever searched for an erotic massage in London, you have seen all of these words, often on the same page and often used as if they meant the same thing.

They do not. Each is a genuinely different experience, with its own pace, technique and atmosphere. Choosing the right one is the difference between a nice hour and an hour you remember for weeks.

As a therapist who offers all of them from my private studio in West Kensington, here is my complete, honest guide to every type of erotic massage — what each one is, how they differ, and how to choose.

## What Is Erotic Massage?

**Erotic massage** is an umbrella term for full-body massage styles in which sensuality is part of the treatment rather than something to be avoided. A clinical massage treats muscles. An erotic massage treats the whole person — slow, deliberate, teasing touch that relaxes you completely and awakens your senses at the same time.

Under that umbrella sit five main styles. They share the same foundations — warm room, warm oil (or gel), unhurried time, complete discretion — but differ in *how* the massage is given and *what* it focuses on.

## The Five Types at a Glance

- **Sensual massage** — slow, hands-led, teasing full-body massage with warm oil. *Best for: first-timers and pure relaxation.*
- **Tantric massage** — breath-led, meditative, built around presence and slowly building energy. *Best for: switching off a busy mind.*
- **Nuru massage** — full-body contact using a special ultra-slippery gel. *Best for: the most physical, playful experience.*
- **Body to body massage** — full-body contact using warm oil, flowing like a classic massage. *Best for: maximum closeness and immersion.*
- **Naturist massage** — client and therapist both nude, in a relaxed, body-positive atmosphere. *Best for: freedom, body confidence and natural intimacy.*

Now let's look at each one properly.

## Sensual Massage — The Foundation

A [sensual massage](/services/sensual-massage) is where most people begin, and where many happily stay. It is a slow, full-body massage with warm oil, given with the hands and forearms, with long, flowing and deliberately teasing strokes.

What makes it *sensual* rather than simply relaxing is the intention: the touch is designed to make you aware of your whole body, not just the knots in your shoulders. The pace is unhurried, the atmosphere is intimate, and nothing feels rushed or mechanical.

**Choose sensual massage if:** you are new to erotic massage, you want the gentlest introduction, or you simply want to be thoroughly, beautifully relaxed.

## Tantric Massage — The Mindful One

[Tantric massage](/services/tantric-massage) is defined less by technique and more by philosophy. Drawing on the ancient practice of tantra, it is built around breath, presence and slowness. A session often begins with a few minutes of guided breathing before any touch at all.

The massage itself is slow, full-body and almost meditative. Rather than rushing anywhere, it lets sensation build gradually and move through the whole body. Clients often describe a state that is deeply relaxed and vividly alive at the same time. My [gentle guide to tantric massage](/blog/what-is-tantric-massage-london) goes deeper into its origins and what a session involves.

**Choose tantric massage if:** your mind never switches off, you are curious about a more mindful kind of touch, or you want a session that feels like a ritual rather than a routine.

## Nuru Massage — The Physical One

[Nuru massage](/services/nuru-massage) comes from Japan, and its name means *slippery* — which tells you most of what you need to know. It uses a special odourless, colourless gel traditionally made from nori seaweed, which creates an extraordinarily smooth, almost weightless glide.

In a nuru massage, the therapist uses her whole body — not just her hands — to glide over yours. It is the most physical and playful style of erotic massage, and the sensation is genuinely unlike anything else. If you are curious about the details, my [full guide to nuru and body to body massage](/blog/nuru-body-to-body-massage-london) explains exactly how it works.

**Choose nuru massage if:** you want the most unusual, immersive sensation, and you love the idea of something completely different from a regular massage.

## Body to Body Massage — The Immersive One

[Body to body massage](/services/body-to-body-massage) is the close cousin of nuru. It is also performed with the whole body rather than the hands alone, but with warm oil instead of gel, so it flows more like a classic sensual massage — just with far more contact.

It is the most intimate style of all, and paradoxically one of the most relaxing: the continuous, enveloping contact quiets the nervous system in a way that isolated touch cannot. I have written a dedicated guide on [what to expect from a body to body erotic massage](/blog/body-to-body-erotic-massage-london).

**Choose body to body if:** you want maximum closeness and immersion, with the warmth and flow of an oil massage.

## Naturist Massage — The Liberating One

**Naturist massage** — sometimes called *nude massage* — is the style people ask about most carefully, so let me explain it plainly. In a naturist massage, both the client and the therapist are nude. That is the defining feature.

The idea comes from naturist philosophy: that the human body is natural, and that removing clothing removes barriers — physical and psychological. Without towels to adjust and nothing to hide behind, self-consciousness tends to melt away surprisingly quickly. What is left is a session that feels relaxed, honest and deeply natural.

The massage itself usually uses warm oil and long, flowing strokes, and it combines beautifully with other styles: a naturist sensual massage, or a naturist tantric session, for example. It is especially popular with clients who value body confidence, freedom and a more equal, natural kind of intimacy.

**Choose naturist massage if:** you feel at ease in your own skin — or want to — and you like the idea of a session without barriers. Simply mention it when you book.

## Tantric vs Nuru vs Naturist: How to Choose

Still undecided? Ask yourself what you want to *feel* when you leave:

- **Calm and restored** → sensual massage
- **Quiet-minded and deeply present** → tantric massage
- **Exhilarated and surprised** → nuru massage
- **Wrapped up and completely let go** → body to body massage
- **Free, confident and unselfconscious** → naturist massage

There is no wrong answer, and you do not have to choose just one. Many of my regular clients alternate between styles, and some prefer a longer session that moves from one into another.
