Paddington is where a great many people begin and end their time in London. The Heathrow Express and the Elizabeth line arrive here, and the streets around the station — Praed Street, Sussex Gardens, the edge of Bayswater — are lined with hotels.

So it is no surprise that "massage near Paddington" is something people search for with a suitcase in one hand. If that is you, here is what is actually available, and what is worth your evening.

## What's Available Near Paddington

### Hotel spas

A few of the larger hotels in and around W2 have spas. They are convenient if you are staying there: you book at reception and take the lift. Expect a standard spa menu, hotel prices, and opening hours that often end earlier than you would like after a long journey.

### Local shops and studios

Walk towards Bayswater and Queensway and you will pass plenty of small massage and beauty shops. Quality varies widely from door to door, and it is hard to judge from the pavement. If you try one, look for clear prices in the window and a clean, calm interior.

### Independent therapists

The third option is an independent therapist with her own studio. You usually need to travel a few stops, but in exchange you get a proper, private, unhurried session with one person who has prepared the room for you.
