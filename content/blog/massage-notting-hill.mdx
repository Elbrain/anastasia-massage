Notting Hill has a character no other part of London quite matches: the pastel terraces, the market on Portobello Road, the mix of people who have lived here for forty years and people who arrived last month.

It does things its own way — and that includes wellness.

## The Notting Hill Approach to Massage

You will not find many big hotel spas in W11. What the neighbourhood has instead is small and individual: boutique wellness studios, yoga and pilates spaces with treatment rooms, and beauty clinics dotted around Westbourne Grove, Notting Hill Gate and down towards Holland Park.

The style tends to be holistic and unhurried, which suits the area. The drawbacks are that the best places book up well ahead, and prices are firmly West London.

## Timing Matters Here

One piece of genuinely local advice: think about when you book.

- **Saturdays** are market day on Portobello Road. The streets are packed and getting anywhere takes twice as long.
- **The August bank holiday weekend** is Carnival. Much of the neighbourhood closes to traffic.
- **Weekday afternoons and evenings** are by far the calmest time to be out and about.

If you are travelling to an appointment — mine or anyone's — a weekday is easier.
