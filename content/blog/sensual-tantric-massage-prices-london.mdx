## What Changes the Price of a Sensual Massage in London

If you compare a few websites you will see very different numbers. These are the things that explain most of the gap.

### 1. The style of massage

Hands-led styles — [sensual](/services/sensual-massage) and [relaxing massage](/services/relaxing-massage) — sit at the lower end. Styles given with the whole body, such as [body to body](/services/body-to-body-massage) and [nuru massage](/services/nuru-massage), and the slower, more involved [tantric massage](/services/tantric-massage), usually cost more. That is true of my own menu and of almost every therapist's.

### 2. Session length

Sessions are usually sold in 30, 60 and 90-minute blocks. The longer the session, the lower the price per minute — which is why an hour is nearly always better value than half an hour.

### 3. In-call or outcall

**In-call** means you visit the therapist's studio. **Outcall** means she travels to your home or hotel. Outcall almost always costs more, because the price has to cover travel time, transport and the risk of a wasted journey. I work in-call only, which is one reason my prices stay where they are. I explain the difference fully in [in-call vs outcall massage in London](/blog/incall-vs-outcall-massage-london).

### 4. Location

Rent in Mayfair or Knightsbridge is not rent in West Kensington, and prices reflect it. A studio a few Tube stops west of the centre can offer the same quality of session for noticeably less — and W14 is only one stop from [Earl's Court](/massage-earls-court) and a direct ride from the West End.

### 5. Independent therapist or agency

When you book through an agency or a parlour, part of what you pay goes to the house. An independent therapist sets her own prices and keeps her own standards. I am fully independent, so what you see on my price list is what the session costs.

## How Does That Compare With an Ordinary Massage?

As a rough guide, an hour of standard massage in London — Swedish, deep tissue and the like — usually falls somewhere between about £50 and £130, depending on the area and the therapist. Specialist sensual and tantric sessions generally cost more than that, and the range between therapists is wide.

Which is exactly why a published price list matters. A clear, written price tells you far more about how a session will go than a low headline number does.

## What Should Be Included

A fair price for a sensual massage should cover everything you need for the session:

- The full, unhurried time you booked — not 50 minutes sold as an hour
- Warm oil, or nuru gel for a nuru massage
- Fresh linen and clean towels
- Use of a shower before and after
- A clean, warm, private room

At my studio, all of that is included in the price. There are no add-ons to buy and nothing to negotiate once you arrive.

## Three Signs You Are Being Quoted Fairly

1. **The price is written down.** If a website will not show prices, ask yourself why.
2. **The price is agreed before you arrive.** You should know the massage, the length and the total cost when you book.
3. **Nothing changes at the door.** A professional does not introduce "extras" halfway through.

If any of those is missing, it is perfectly reasonable to look elsewhere. I cover the etiquette on both sides in [your first sensual massage: what to expect](/blog/first-sensual-massage-london-what-to-expect).
