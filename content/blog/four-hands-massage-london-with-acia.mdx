There is a question I hear from regular clients more than almost any other: *what is a four hands massage actually like?* And there is a shorter answer and a longer one.

The short answer: it is the most immersive massage experience I know how to give.

The longer answer involves introducing you to someone — because a real four hands session is only ever as good as the two therapists giving it.

## What Four Hands Actually Feels Like

A four hands massage is exactly what it sounds like: two therapists working on you simultaneously, in mirrored, synchronised movements. Long strokes travel down both sides of your body at once; four warm hands move in rhythm rather than taking turns.

Something curious happens in the first ten minutes. With a single therapist, your mind quietly follows the touch — where it is now, where it is going next. With two therapists moving in sync, that tracking becomes impossible. The mind simply gives up and lets go. Clients describe it as being *enveloped*, and honestly, that is the best word for it. It is the closest thing to switching your thoughts off entirely that I have found in this work.

If a classic [sensual massage](/services/sensual-massage) is a conversation between one pair of hands and your body, four hands is a choir.

## How Our Four Hands Sessions Work

We hold four hands sessions at my private studio in West Kensington (W14) on selected days, when both of our diaries align. The session itself flows like any of my treatments — warm oil, soft light, no clock-watching — except that everything is doubled: the preparation, the attention, and of course the hands.

A few practical notes:

- **Book ahead.** Two diaries need to line up, so a few days' notice makes everything easier. A quick [WhatsApp message](/booking) is all it takes.
- **Allow enough time.** Four hands works best at 60 minutes or more — the immersion deserves room to build.
- **First-timers are welcome.** If you have never tried even a regular massage with me, you can absolutely start with four hands. Though be warned: it sets the bar rather high.

Between sessions together, we each see our own clients — me in [West Kensington](/massage-west-london), Acia in Bayswater. Two studios, one standard.
